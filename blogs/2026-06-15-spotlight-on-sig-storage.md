Interview with Xing Yang, Co-Chair of SIG Storage at VMware by Broadcom, covering the group's work on persistent data and volume management in Kubernetes. Recent features highlighted include VolumeGroupSnapshot (GA in v1.36), CSI Changed Block Tracking (Beta in v1.36), the Container Object Storage Interface (COSI), and VolumeAttributesClass (GA in v1.34). It also discusses stateful-workload challenges like data gravity and Day 2 complexity, plus future storage trends for AI/ML such as intelligent data management and data-aware scheduling.
